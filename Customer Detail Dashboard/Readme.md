
# 📊 Customer Detail Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

The **Customer Detail Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer behavior, sales performance, revenue, and profitability. It provides insights into customer demographics, purchasing patterns, and geographical profit distribution through KPI cards, charts, maps, and interactive filters.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, revenue, and gross profit.
- Identify the top five customers by orders, revenue, and profit.
- Understand profitability across different customer age groups.
- Compare profit contributions by gender and marital status.
- Analyze country-wise and city-wise profitability.
- Identify the most and least profitable cities.
- Support data-driven business decisions through interactive visualizations.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Top 5 Customers Analysis

Displays the top five customers based on:
- Quantity ordered
- Total revenue
- Gross profit

This analysis helps identify valuable customers and understand their contribution to business performance.

### 2. Profit by Customer Age Group

Analyzes profit across three customer age groups:

- **0–30 years:** 51% contribution
- **31–50 years:** 32% contribution
- **51+ years:** 18% contribution

This visualization helps identify the age groups contributing most to profitability.

### 3. Profit by Customer Country

Displays geographical profit distribution across Canada, Mexico, and the USA.

- **USA:** 61% contribution
- **Mexico:** 32% contribution
- **Canada:** 7% contribution

The map helps compare country-level performance and explore geographical business trends.

### 4. Profit Analysis by Gender

Compares profit contributions by gender.

- Female customers: 37%
- Male customers: 63%

This visualization highlights the distribution of profit across customer groups.

### 5. Customer Profit by Marital Status

Compares the profit contribution of married and single customers.

- Married customers: 31%
- Single customers: 69%

This analysis supports customer segmentation and marketing decisions.

### 6. Top 5 Profitable Cities

Highlights the five cities with the highest profit:

- Hidalgo
- Merida
- Spokane
- Orizaba
- Camacho

### 7. Least 5 Profitable Cities

Highlights the five cities with the lowest profit:

- Mill Valley
- San Francisco
- San Carlos
- Redwood City
- Colma

These insights help identify geographical areas for further business analysis.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes the following slicers:

- **Month Name:** Filter data from January to December.
- **Year:** Select 2020, 2021, or 2022.
- **Customer Country:** Filter by Canada, Mexico, or the USA.

These filters allow users to explore the data dynamically.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX:** Calculated measures and KPI creation.
- **Data Modeling:** Establishing relationships between tables.
- **Charts and Maps:** Visual representation of customer and profitability data.

## ⚙️ Working of the Dashboard

1. **Data Collection:** Import customer, sales, product, and transaction data.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Use Power Query to clean and transform the dataset.
3. **Data Modeling:** Establish relationships between relevant tables.
4. **DAX Calculations:** Create measures for revenue, profit, order quantities, and customer counts.
5. **Visualization:** Build KPI cards, bar charts, doughnut charts, and maps.
6. **Interactive Filtering:** Apply slicers to analyze specific months, years, and countries.
7. **Insight Generation:** Use the dashboard to identify customer trends and business opportunities.
