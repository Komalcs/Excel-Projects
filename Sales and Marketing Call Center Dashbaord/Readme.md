
# 📊 Sales and Marketing Call Center Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

The **Sales and Marketing Call Center Dashboard** provides insights into call center performance, agent productivity, call outcomes, customer satisfaction, product discussions, and geographical call distribution. It helps businesses monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and identify opportunities to improve customer service and sales performance.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze total, successful, failed, and abandoned calls.
- Evaluate call agent performance and productivity.
- Monitor customer satisfaction and call duration.
- Identify the major reasons for abandoned calls.
- Analyze monthly call volume trends.
- Understand customer distribution by gender and state.
- Compare successful and failed calls across product categories.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Calls:** 80,100
- **Successful Calls:** 33,465
- **Failed Calls:** 28,781
- **Abandoned Calls:** 17,854
- **Male Customer Calls:** 55,904
- **Female Customer Calls:** 24,196
- **Average Customer Rating:** 4.3
- **Average Call Duration:** 15.8 minutes

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Total Calls by Agent
Displays the total calls handled by each agent to compare productivity and identify high-performing employees.

### 2. Call Outcome Analysis
Shows the selected agent's call outcomes and success percentage, helping evaluate call handling performance.

### 3. Reasons for Abandoned Calls
Identifies the primary reasons for abandoned calls, including:
- Technical Issues
- Long Wait Time
- Customer Hung Up
- Agent Not Available

### 4. Monthly Call Trend
Visualizes monthly call volumes to identify peak and minimum call periods and support workforce planning.

### 5. Geographical Call Distribution
Displays customer call distribution across different states in the United States to identify regions generating more calls.

### 6. Customer Gender Analysis
Compares calls from male and female customers to understand customer engagement patterns.

### 7. Product Discussion Analysis
Compares successful and failed calls across different product categories:
- Travel Package
- Loans
- Internet Package
- Insurance
- Electronics

### 8. Agent Performance Analysis
Displays the selected agent's average customer rating and average call duration to assess service quality and efficiency.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

- **Call Agent:** Select an individual agent to analyze their performance.
- **Call Time:** Filter call data by Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.

## 💡 Key Insights

- The dashboard records **80,100 total calls**.
- Successful calls represent approximately **41.78%** of total calls.
- Failed calls represent approximately **35.93%** of total calls.
- Abandoned calls represent approximately **22.29%** of total calls.
- Male customers account for approximately **69.8%** of calls, while female customers account for approximately **30.2%**.
- Technical issues are the leading recorded reason for abandoned calls.
- October shows the minimum call volume, while December shows the maximum in the displayed monthly trend.
- Agent-level and product-level analysis helps identify areas for improving call center performance.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** – KPI calculations and analytical measures.
- **Data Visualization** – Bar charts, doughnut charts, line charts, KPI cards, and map visualizations.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection:** Import call center data.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Handle missing values and correct data types.
3. **Data Transformation:** Prepare and organize the data using Power Query.
4. **Data Modeling:** Establish relationships between relevant tables.
5. **DAX Calculations:** Create measures for call counts, success rates, ratings, and call duration.
6. **Dashboard Development:** Create visualizations, KPI cards, and interactive filters.
7. **Data Analysis:** Interpret results and identify opportunities for improvement.
