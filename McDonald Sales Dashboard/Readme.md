# 🍔 McDonald's Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is a **McDonald's Sales Dashboard** created to analyze and visualize key business performance metrics, including sales, orders, new customers, customer satisfaction, monthly sales trends, and sales by country.

The dashboard provides a clear and interactive overview of McDonald's sales performance for the **2025–2026** period.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Track total orders and new customers.
- Monitor monthly sales trends.
- Compare sales performance across different countries.
- Analyze customer satisfaction across different service factors.
- Present important business KPIs in a visually appealing dashboard.
- Identify trends and patterns in sales data.

## 💡 Key Insights

- The dashboard provides an overview of sales, orders, customers, and customer satisfaction.
- **Argentina** has the highest displayed sales value at **953.3**.
- **Chile** has the lowest displayed sales value at **253.6**.
- **Availability** has the highest customer satisfaction score at **95%**.
- **Hygiene** also has a high satisfaction score of **93%**.
- **Service** and **Speed** have comparatively lower satisfaction scores of **53%** and **54%**.
- The monthly sales chart helps identify changes in sales performance throughout the year.
