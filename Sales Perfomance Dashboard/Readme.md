# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Performance Dashboard** is an interactive sales analytics dashboard designed to monitor sales performance, product contribution, payment methods, monthly trends, and seller performance.

The dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of key business metrics, helping users understand sales patterns and identify top-performing products and sellers.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Track overall sales performance.
- Monitor total units sold.
- Analyze monthly sales and order trends.
- Identify top-selling products.
- Understand product-wise contribution to total sales.
- Compare payment methods.
- Analyze seller performance.
- Identify seasonal sales patterns and trends.

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Sales Trends Over Time

The monthly sales analysis shows the following performance:

| Month | Sales |
|---|---:|
| April | ₹2.09M |
| May | ₹2.23M |
| June | ₹2.14M |
| July | ₹1.82M |
| August | ₹3.95M |

Sales remained relatively stable from April to June, decreased in July, and then increased significantly during August and September.
