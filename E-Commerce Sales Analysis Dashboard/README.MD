# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

## 📌 Overview

This project is an **interactive E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** built entirely in **Microsoft Excel**. The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance, profitability, customer orders, and category-wise analysis using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Excel formulas.

It enables users to monitor key business metrics, identify top-performing categories, analyze regional sales, and compare year-over-year (YoY) growth.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Monitor profit and profit margin.
- Track order quantity and number of orders.
- Compare Year-over-Year (YoY) growth.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Analyze sales distribution across states.
- Discover the best-selling sub-categories.
- Filter data dynamically using slicers.

# 📊 Dashboard Features

## 1. Sales KPI Cards

Displays important business metrics:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Quantity Sold
- Number of Orders
- Profit Margin
- YoY Growth Indicators
- Mini Trend (Sparkline)

---

## 2. Sales & Profit Analysis

A monthly comparison chart showing:

- Monthly Sales
- Monthly Profit
- Business trends
- Seasonal performance

This helps identify:

- Peak sales months
- Low-performing months
- Profit fluctuations

---

## 3. Category-wise Profit

Waterfall Chart displaying profit contribution from:

- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

Quickly identifies which category contributes the highest profit.

---

## 4. Category-wise Sales Share

A Doughnut Chart visualizing the percentage contribution of each product category.

Categories include:

- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

---

## 5. Sales by State

A filled map visualizing geographical sales distribution across the United States.

It helps identify:

- High-performing states
- Low-performing states
- Regional sales trends

---

## 6. Top 10 Sub-Categories

Horizontal bar chart displaying best-performing product sub-categories.

Examples include:

- Machines
- Storage
- Phones
- Binders
- Chairs

Useful for inventory and marketing decisions.

---

## 7. Interactive Filters (Slicers)

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard using:

- Year
- Region
- Segment

Available Regions:

- Central
- East
- South
- West

Customer Segments:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office
