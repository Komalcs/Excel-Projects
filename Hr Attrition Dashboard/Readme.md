# HR Attrition Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
The **HR Attrition Dashboard** is a data visualization project designed to analyze employee attrition and understand workforce trends. It provides insights into employee demographics, job roles, education, business travel, gender, age groups, departments, performance, and work distance.

The dashboard helps HR professionals identify attrition patterns and make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall employee attrition and active employee statistics.
- Identify job roles with the highest attrition.
- Understand attrition patterns across education levels.
- Compare attrition across age groups and genders.
- Analyze employee attrition based on business travel.
- Examine department-wise attrition.
- Explore employee performance and work distance categories.
- Support better workforce planning and retention strategies.

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### 1. Employee Attrition Overview
The organization has 2,925 employees, including 492 employees who have left and 2,433 active employees. The attrition rate is 17%, while 83% of employees remain active.

### 2. Attrition by Job Role
The dashboard highlights the following job roles with high attrition:

- Laboratory Technician: 130
- Sales Executive: 113
- Research Scientist: 99
- Sales Representative: 68
- Human Resources: 24
- Manufacturing Director: 23

Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives have the highest attrition counts among the displayed roles.

### 3. Attrition by Education
The education-wise attrition distribution is:

- Life Sciences: 190
- Medical: 132
- Marketing: 70
- Technical Degree: 64
- Other: 22
- Human Resources: 14

Life Sciences has the highest attrition count among the displayed education categories.

### 4. Attrition by Age Group and Gender
The dashboard compares attrition across five age groups:

- 18–25
- 26–35
- 36–45
- 46–55
- 56+

The 26–35 age group shows the highest attrition in the displayed chart. Gender comparisons help identify differences in attrition patterns across age groups.

### 5. Attrition by Business Travel
Employees are categorized into three business travel groups:

- Non-Travel
- Travel Frequently
- Travel Rarely

This analysis helps HR teams explore the relationship between business travel frequency and employee attrition.

### 6. Attrition by Gender
The dashboard shows the following gender-wise attrition percentages:

- Female Attrition: 37%
- Male Attrition: 63%

Male employees account for a larger share of attrition in the displayed breakdown.

### 7. Department-Wise Attrition
The department-wise attrition counts are:

- HR: 24
- R&D: 186
- Sales: 282

Sales has the highest attrition count, followed by R&D and HR.

### 8. Attrition by Work Distance
The dashboard categorizes work distance as follows:

- Very-far: 94 employees
- Near-by: 296 employees
- Far: 102 employees

The near-by category has the highest displayed count.
