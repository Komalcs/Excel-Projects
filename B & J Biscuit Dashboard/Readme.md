# 🍪 B&J Biscuit Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **B&J Biscuit Dashboard** is a data visualization project designed to analyze biscuit sales, revenue, profitability, customer behavior, and geographical sales performance.

This project contains **two dashboards in one file**, providing a comprehensive overview of business performance through key performance indicators (KPIs), charts, and interactive filters.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance and profitability.
- Monitor total revenue, profit, and cost of goods sold.
- Identify the most profitable brands and locations.
- Analyze monthly, quarterly, and weekly revenue trends.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Compare revenue across different age groups and genders.
- Analyze revenue distribution by payment method.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboards include the following KPIs:

- **Quantity Sold:** 17,152
- **Total COGS:** $203,081
- **Total Revenue:** $321,937
- **Total Profit:** $118,856

## 📈 Dashboard 1: Sales Performance and Profitability

### Key Features

- **Quarterly Revenue Analysis:** Compares revenue across four quarters.
- **Monthly Revenue Trends:** Tracks revenue from January to December 2024.
- **Weekly Revenue Analysis:** Compares revenue across different days of the week.
- **Weekday vs. Weekend Analysis:** Evaluates revenue generated on weekdays and weekends.
- **Profitability Analysis:** Identifies the most profitable brand, location, and customer.
- **Salesperson Analysis:** Highlights the top-performing salesperson.
- **Customer Age Group Analysis:** Identifies the most profitable age group.
- **Interactive Filters:** Enables analysis by location and payment method.

### Key Insights

- **Most Profitable Brand:** Shortbread
- **Most Profitable Location:** San Antonio
- **Most Profitable Customer:** Juan Porter
- **Top Salesperson:** April Watkins
- **Most Profitable Age Group:** 60–74

## 📊 Dashboard 2: Revenue Distribution and Customer Insights

### Key Features

- **Revenue by Product Price Category:** Compares revenue from different product price categories.
- **Revenue by Age Group:** Analyzes revenue contributions from different customer age groups.
- **Top 5 Customers:** Identifies the customers contributing the most revenue.
- **Geographical Revenue Distribution:** Compares revenue across different cities.
- **Revenue by Gender:** Visualizes revenue distribution across gender categories.
- **Revenue by Payment Method:** Compares revenue generated through cash, debit cards, mobile payments, and credit cards.
- **Interactive Filters:** Allows users to explore data by location, age group, and payment method.
