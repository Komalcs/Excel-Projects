# 👟 Adidas Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

The **Adidas Sales Analytics Dashboard** provides insights into sales performance, revenue trends, product categories, customer preferences, sales channels, and geographical distribution across the United States.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall Adidas sales performance.
- Track total revenue, quantity ordered, and transactions.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Compare purchasing patterns across male and female customers.
- Analyze sales performance across different US states.
- Identify peak sales months and seasonal trends.
- Compare sales channels across different regions.
- Analyze weekday revenue trends.
- Identify opportunities for business growth.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Sales Channel Analysis

Analyzes sales performance across three channels:

- In-store
- Online
- Outlet

**Key Insights:**
- Compare revenue contributions across sales channels.
- Identify better-performing sales channels.
- Understand channel performance across different regions.

### 2. Product Revenue Analysis

Compares revenue generated by the following product categories:

- Apparel
- Street Footwear
- Athletic Footwear

**Key Insights:**
- Identify products contributing the most to revenue.
- Compare revenue across product categories.
- Analyze purchasing patterns across male and female customers.

### 3. Geographical Sales Analysis

The geographical map displays sales distribution across different US states.

**Key Insights:**
- Identify high-performing states.
- Compare geographical sales performance.
- Discover potential opportunities for regional expansion.
- Support targeted marketing strategies.

### 4. Monthly Revenue Trends

The line chart compares monthly revenue trends for 2020 and 2021.

**Key Insights:**
- Identify peak-performing months.
- Compare revenue between the two years.
- Understand seasonal fluctuations in sales.
- Support future sales planning.

### 5. Regional Sales and Channel Analysis

Compares sales channel contributions across five regions:

- West
- Southeast
- South
- Northeast
- Midwest

**Key Insights:**
- Compare In-store, Online, and Outlet sales.
- Identify regional differences in channel performance.
- Discover opportunities to improve regional sales.
- Support region-specific marketing strategies.

### 6. Weekday Revenue Analysis

The column chart compares revenue performance across the seven days of the week.

**Key Insights:**
- Identify days with stronger sales performance.
- Compare weekday and weekend trends.
- Support promotional campaign planning.
- Improve inventory and sales planning.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes the following filters:

- **Month:** Analyze sales for selected months.
- **Sales Channel:** Filter by In-store, Online, or Outlet.
- **Product:** Explore specific product categories.
- **Year:** Compare sales performance for 2020 and 2021.

These filters allow users to explore the data dynamically and analyze specific aspects of sales performance.
