# 📊 Sales Annual Report 2022

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Annual Report 2022** is an interactive sales dashboard designed to analyze and visualize business performance across different dimensions such as sales, orders, customer demographics, states, sales channels, and order status.

The dashboard provides a clear overview of **sales trends, customer behavior, regional performance, channel contribution, and order fulfillment** for 2022.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze monthly **sales and order trends**.
- Compare sales contribution between **men and women**.
- Understand order distribution across different **age groups and genders**.
- Identify the **top-performing states** based on sales.
- Analyze sales contribution from different **order channels**.
- Monitor the distribution of different **order statuses**.
- Provide interactive filters for **Category, Month, and Channel**.
- Present business insights through easy-to-understand visualizations.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Orders vs Sales

A combination chart showing the monthly relationship between **sales amount and number of orders**.

- Sales are represented using columns.
- Orders are represented using a line.
- The chart covers all months from **January to December 2022**.
- March shows a noticeable peak in orders and sales.
- Sales and orders generally decline toward the end of the year.

---

### 2. Sales: Men vs Women

A pie chart showing the distribution of sales between male and female customers.

| Gender | Sales Contribution |
|---|---:|
| Women | 64% |
| Men | 36% |

The dashboard shows that women account for a larger share of total sales compared with men.

---

### 3. Orders: Age vs Gender

This visualization compares orders across **Adult, Senior, and Young** customer groups.

| Age Group | Men | Women |
|---|---:|---:|
| Adult | 17% | 31% |
| Senior | 7% | 12% |
| Young | 12% | 21% |

The visualization shows that women have a higher order contribution than men across all displayed age groups.

---

### 4. Sales: Top 5 States

The dashboard identifies the five states with the highest sales.

| Rank | State | Sales |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Maharashtra | 2.99M |
| 2 | Karnataka | 2.65M |
| 3 | Uttar Pradesh | 2.10M |
| 4 | Telangana | 1.71M |
| 5 | Tamil Nadu | 1.68M |

Maharashtra has the highest sales among the five states displayed.

---

### 5. Orders Channels

The dashboard compares order contribution across different sales channels.

| Channel | Order Contribution |
|---|---:|
| Amazon | 36% |
| Myntra | 23% |
| Flipkart | 22% |
| Ajio | 6% |
| Nalli | 5% |
| Meesho | 4% |

Amazon represents the largest share among the displayed sales channels.

---

### 6. Order Status

A pie chart presents the distribution of order fulfillment status.

| Order Status | Percentage |
|---|---:|
| Delivered | 93% |
| Returned | 4% |
| Cancelled | 2% |
| Refunded | 1% |

The majority of orders shown in the dashboard were delivered.

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Women contribute 64% of sales**, compared with 36% from men.
- **Maharashtra** records the highest sales among the top five states shown.
- **Amazon contributes 36% of orders**, making it the largest displayed channel.
- **Delivered orders account for 93%** of the order-status distribution.
- Adult women represent the largest percentage in the Age vs Gender visualization at **31%**.
- March shows a prominent peak in the monthly order trend.
- Sales and order volumes show a decline during the later months of the year.
