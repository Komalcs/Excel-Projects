# 🛒 DMart Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **DMart Sales Dashboard** is an interactive **Microsoft Excel dashboard** created to analyze and visualize sales performance across different categories, sub-categories, regions, customer segments, shipping modes, and states.

The dashboard provides key business insights through interactive charts, KPI cards, slicers, and a date timeline.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Track important sales KPIs.
- Compare sales across different regions.
- Identify top-performing categories and sub-categories.
- Analyze sales based on shipping modes.
- Understand sales contribution from different customer segments.
- Provide state-wise sales analysis.
- Enable interactive filtering for better decision-making.

## 📈 Dashboard Components

### 1. Category-Wise Sales

The **Category-Wise Sales** donut chart shows the sales contribution of different product categories.

It helps identify which major product categories generate the highest sales.

---

### 2. Sub-Category-Wise Sales

The **Sub-Category-Wise Sales** horizontal bar chart compares sales across different product sub-categories.

The dashboard includes sub-categories such as:

- Phones
- Chairs
- Storage
- Tables
- Binders
- Machines
- Accessories
- Copiers
- Bookcases
- Appliances
- Furnishings
- Paper
- Supplies
- Art
- Envelopes
- Labels
- Fasteners

This visualization makes it easy to compare product-level sales performance.

---

### 3. Region-Wise Sales

The **Region-Wise Sales** chart compares sales across four regions:

- West
- East
- South
- Central

The **West region** has the highest sales contribution among the regions shown in the dashboard.

---

### 4. Ship-Mode-Wise Sales

The **Ship-Mode-Wise Sales** chart analyzes sales according to different shipping methods:

- First Class
- Same Day
- Second Class
- Standard Class

The dashboard shows that **Standard Class** contributes the highest sales.

---

### 5. Segment-Wise Sales

The **Segment-Wise Sales** donut chart represents sales distribution across different customer segments.

This analysis helps understand which customer segment contributes the most to overall sales.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

### 📅 Ship Date

The **Ship Date Timeline** allows users to filter the dashboard according to different time periods and months.

### 📍 State

The **State Slicer** allows users to select specific states and analyze their sales performance.

### 📦 Sub-Category

The **Sub-Category Slicer** allows users to filter the dashboard for specific products or sub-categories.

These interactive controls make the dashboard dynamic and easy to explore.
