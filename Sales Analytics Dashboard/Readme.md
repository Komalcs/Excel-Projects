# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

The **Sales Performance Dashboard** is an interactive sales analytics dashboard designed to provide a clear overview of sales performance, product contribution, payment methods, seasonal trends, and seller performance.

The dashboard helps transform raw sales data into meaningful insights through **KPIs, charts, slicers, and trend analysis**, making it easier to understand business performance and identify important sales patterns.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance.
- Track total sales and total units sold.
- Analyze monthly sales and order trends.
- Identify top-selling products.
- Compare product contribution to total sales.
- Analyze payment mode distribution.
- Compare sales performance across sellers.
- Identify seasonal sales patterns and trends.

---
